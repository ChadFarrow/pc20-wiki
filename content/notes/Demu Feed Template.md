# Demu Feed Template

The Demu Feed Template is a set of ready-made RSS files and instructions for artists self-publishing decentralized music.

## Why it matters

A music feed with a working value block is not conceptually hard, but it is fiddly enough that the XML is where most artists stop. Handing someone a correct feed to fill in removes the step where [[Value 4 Value]] music dies of namespace syntax.

## How it works

The repo carries a clean `feed.xml` to build from and an annotated `feed-with-comments.xml` explaining what each tag does, covering the [[Podcast Namespace]] elements a music release needs. The materials are themselves offered on a value-for-value basis: artists are asked to leave the project's [[Splits]] entry intact in feeds they generate from the template.
