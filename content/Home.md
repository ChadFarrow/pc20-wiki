## How this vault works

- One concept per note. Title the note the thing itself: `Keysend`, not `Notes on keysend`.
- Every note opens with a one-sentence definition before anything else.
- Link the first mention of any concept, even if the note does not exist yet. Unresolved links are the writing queue.
- Folders are storage, not structure. Navigation lives in MOCs and links.
- Frontmatter carries `type`, `status`, and `tags` so notes stay queryable.

## Conventions

`type`: concept | protocol | spec | tool | project | source | person
`status`: seed (stub) | growing (in progress) | evergreen (holds up on reread)

## Maintenance

- Weekly: open the unresolved links view, promote the useful ones to seeds.
- Monthly: pick three seeds and grow them.
- When a note passes roughly 500 words and covers two ideas, split it.
